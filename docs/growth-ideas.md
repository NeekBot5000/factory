# Why build a bigger farm? (pitch)

The problem: once a small tree farm works, there's no reason to grow it. Each idea below gives the player a reason
to want *more* wood, *faster*, or *different* wood, without a goals checklist. Grouped by the kind of pull it creates.
★ marks my favourites.

## 1. Things to spend huge amounts of wood on (sinks)

1. ★ **Town projects**: a town down the road commissions big builds in stages (a covered bridge, a sawmill museum, a
   giant treehouse, a timber stadium). Each stage needs hundreds of planks; you see it go up in the world, built from
   *your* wood (each project uses the colours of the species you sent).
2. ★ **Expand your land**: buy plots in steps; bigger plots fit bigger farms, and some plots have rare trees or a river.
3. **Upgrade the cabin** from cabin to lodge to timber mansion, each stage made of planks.
4. **A mega-project at the end**: a giant timber tower / cathedral / log flume ride that eats thousands of planks and
   ends in a celebration.
5. **Bigger trucks and a second truck**: more you can ship at once, but they cost wood to build.
6. **Machine upgrades that cost products**: faster saw blades (iron), bigger hoppers (planks), faster belts.

## 2. Customers who want more than you can make (demand)

7. ★ **Contracts on the phone**: "40 oak planks by Friday", "6 cedar logs over 3 m". Bigger customers want bigger
   orders and pay bonuses; ignoring them is fine.
8. **Customers who get bigger as you serve them**: the furniture shop becomes a factory and its orders grow.
9. **Market prices that move**: when oak is high, a big oak farm pays off; when it crashes, switch species.
10. **A daily truck quota bonus**: ship over $X in one truckload for a bonus (encourages automating the loading).
11. **Rare orders** for exotic woods you can only grow (no wild ones), so you build special farms for them.

## 3. Better wood that only big setups can make (quality and chains)

12. ★ **Longer production chains**: log → planks → dried planks (kiln) → furniture (workbench) → each step worth more.
    A bigger farm feeds a longer, more valuable chain.
13. **Tree species with different values** you unlock and grow from rare seeds (cedar, cherry, rainbow eucalyptus...).
14. **Grafting and mutations**: rare saplings appear from big farms (1 in 50), and you breed them into valuable variants.
15. **Clean, stripped logs pay more** (already true for the sawmill): automate debarking to get the bonus at scale.
16. **Old trees are worth more**: let some grow longer for thicker, pricier trunks (a reason for patience *and* space).

## 4. Automation that only pays off at scale (more machines)

17. ★ **Debarker drum**: logs tumble through and come out stripped (bark falls out into a chipper below).
18. ★ **Grabber arm**: picks things off a belt and drops them somewhere else (sorting, feeding machines).
19. **Sorter belt**: one belt that sends logs left and bark right, or by species, with a physical sample in its slot.
20. **Splitter and merger belts**.
21. **Log loader crane**: lifts logs from a pile onto the truck.
22. **Harvester**: drives a row of trees, fells and strips them (late game, very satisfying).
23. **Kiln**: dries planks over time (worth more).
24. **Furniture bench**: planks become chairs/tables (powered or by hand).
25. **Paper press**: mulch → paper.
26. **Water wheel** (needs a river plot), **steam engine** (burns charcoal), **solar** later.
27. **Charcoal kiln**: wood → charcoal (better generator fuel).
28. **Item counter display** and **chest/storage hopper** for buffering.
29. **Wireless transmitter/receiver** pairs for big farms where wires get long.
30. **Rail carts** to bring logs from far plots to the mill.

## 5. Showing off (pride and identity)

31. **A company sign that grows** at the cabin gate as your business grows (no numbers: it gets bigger and fancier).
32. **Trophy wall and museum**: one of every species, your biggest log slice, the Legendary Tree's stump.
33. **Records board**: biggest truckload, most trees in one day.
34. **Photo mode** for sharing your factory.
35. **Lights and decoration** for a factory that looks great at night.

## 6. Pressure and surprises (light, never punishing)

36. **Storms** knock trees over (free logs) or knock things off belts; lightning can set a tree on fire.
37. **Seasons**: no growth in winter, so you stockpile in autumn; sap only in spring.
38. **Night shift**: machines keep running while you sleep in the hammock (skip time = production).
39. **Pests (no animals): rot or fungus** that spreads between crowded trees, so farms need spacing or sprinklers.

## 7. Co-op (the big one, if you ever do it)

40. Friends each run part of the chain (felling, mill, trucks) and the farm *needs* to be bigger to keep everyone busy.

## Suggested first three

If I were picking: **contracts (7)** for steady goals, **town projects (1)** as the long-term sink, and **the
debarker + grabber arm (17, 18)** so bigger farms can actually be automated end to end.
