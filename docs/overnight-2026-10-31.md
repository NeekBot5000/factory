## The headline findings

1. **The verb sells it.** PowerWash Simulator (12M+ players) proves one satisfying, relaxing action is enough. Timber's
   is peeling bark in sheets; polish it until it's a perfect 10-second clip.
2. **Co-op is the biggest multiplier**, and the hardest thing on the list. Co-op games made ~$4.1B on Steam in H1 2025.
   Hydroneer (the closest game in spirit, ~500k sold) gave up on true online play because of physics sync; Roadside
   Research made it work by designing for it from the start. A staged plan is in the launch kit.
3. **A direct rival launched in August 2026: Chop Chop Inc.** (first-person furniture-business automation, comedic,
   single-player, Very Positive). Timber's angle should be *co-op forest work with real physics*, not *wacky factory*.
4. **Wishlists and the demo decide the launch.** Steam page up early, daily short clips, a demo weeks before Next Fest.
   Recommended target: the **June 14-21, 2027 Next Fest** (registration closes 25 April 2027).
5. **Make it a real PC game first**: native build, menus, saves, Steam Cloud, controller support, achievements.
6. **Localise the store page**: Simplified Chinese is the biggest language on Steam (33.7% in 2024).

  rows in the species sheet are marked as unchecked).

## Limits

- This session could search the web but **couldn't open most pages** (howtomarketagame.com, gamedeveloper.com, the
  Steam store, vaporlens and others were blocked), so most facts come from search summaries of those pages. Every
  claim links its source. Allowing those domains in the environment's network settings would allow a deeper pass.
- Sales figures are developer statements or press; third-party estimates vary wildly. Wishlist thresholds are
  marketers' rules of thumb, not Valve rules.
- Nothing was published or posted anywhere. No game code was changed.
