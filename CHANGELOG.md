# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - first itch.io release

Everything since the prototype, in short:

- **Bark and trees:** peel outer and inner bark in sheets with 30+ tools, fell trees, cut logs, the Legendary Tree.
- **Selling:** the log truck (with Big Truck and Log Hauler upgrades), the TimberMart shop on the cabin computer.
- **Building:** the hammer builds storage, furniture, decorations (worth far more than their materials) and machines.
- **Machines:** power (crank, windmill, generator, battery), switches, seven logic blocks, sensors, lamps,
  conveyors, pistons, rotators, sawmill, chipper, kiln, smelter, assembler, grabber arms, sorters, storage crates,
  wireless, tree farm machines and drill rigs.
- **Rails and carts:** rails and curves you lay by hand, minecarts 1-4 squares long carrying machines on their
  decks, Go/Reverse, rail layer and lifter, switches, buffers, unloaders, level and cart sensors.
- **Drones:** three sizes, each lifting only so much, steered by five wireless dials.
- **Power sockets:** every machine has one; wires plug in, battery cells click in.
- **Mining:** dig down with pickaxes and drills; copper from 1.5 m, iron from 5 m.
- **Growth:** buy more land, company sign, trophy wall.
- **Menus:** title screen, starting tutorial, pause menu with settings, fullscreen (F11), credits, quit.
- **Fixed for release:** New Game now really starts a new world (it used to show the title again and keep the
  old builds).
