Peel it. Fell it. Build something bigger.

A first-person lumberjack game where everything is a real object. Peel bark off trees in sheets, carry it to your truck and send it to the mill.

Start with bare hands. Buy axes, saws and drills, fell trees, build furniture worth more than the wood. Then automate: windmills, belts, sawmills, smelters, rails, drones and oil, from one cabin to a factory.

NEW
- Golden tree every 15 minutes: 5x bark, race the server for it
- Daily jobs on your TimberPhone, with bonuses
- Daily reward: more each day in a row, up to $105
- Rush hour: double pay at the mill for the first 5 minutes of every hour
- Wood of the day pays 1.5x at the mill
- Play with a friend: +10% at the mill
- Leaderboards and badges
